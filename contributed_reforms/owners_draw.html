Owners’ draw SECA reform¶

This contributed reform brings eligible pass-through profits into the SECA OASDI base. It is off by default. The example configuration phases in a share of 0.1 in 2027, rising by 0.1 annually to 1 in 2036. These are custom example settings, not enacted law or a complete implementation of an OACT provision. Population scoring uses the tax side only.

Parameters¶

All settings are under gov.contrib.ssa.owners_draw.

-1

Derive the linear share; an annual value in [0, 1] overrides it.

Derive the standard SECA factor; a value in (0, 1] overrides it.

Use nonpassive income as the participation proxy.

Also tax adjusted draw earnings at the uncapped SECA HI rate.

benefit_credit

true

Add adjusted annual draw earnings to the existing AIME earnings history before its annual cap.

Apply the existing section 164(f) deductible share to added SECA.

room_cap_basis

Preserve the current-law OASDI cap; 1 removes the draw ceiling.

The derived share is min(max((year - start_year + 1) / phase_in_years, 0), 1). Activation and start-year checks still apply to explicit share overrides. The current-law cap is copied before user reforms, so removing the general payroll cap does not silently remove this draw ceiling.

Input contract and signed losses¶

For each Person, define total signed entity income T = s_corp_income + partnership_income, passive subset Q = passive_partnership_s_corp_income, and already-SE partnership subset K = partnership_self_employment_net_earnings. Both Q and K are already in T; K is a gross source amount before SECA’s net-earnings adjustment.

With disjoint passive and already-SE subsets, the signed active residual is G = T - Q - K. If both subsets are nonzero, either supply owners_draw_subsets_are_disjoint: true or the signed intersection in owners_draw_passive_se_overlap. The latter yields K_active = K - overlap and G = T - Q - K_active. Ambiguous classifications raise an error. If either subset is zero, disjointness follows without an additional assertion. With material_participation_only: false, include the passive subset and subtract the entire already-SE K instead.

Signed profits and losses net within the owner’s supplied entity totals before flooring the residual. This is an approximation without entity-level detail. A negative residual does not offset wages or existing SE earnings. 26 U.S.C. 1402(a) provides the comparable income-minus-deductions approach for net SE earnings; its existing exclusions are not a legal basis for this contributed expansion. Nonpassive status is a material-participation proxy and does not identify every legal limited-partner class.

Gross draw, adjusted earnings, and cap priority¶

The planned gross draw is D_planned = share * max(G, 0). The default netting factor is the existing model expression:

q = 1 - gov.irs.ald.misc.employer_share
        * (gov.irs.self_employment.rate.social_security
           + gov.irs.self_employment.rate.medicare)

Current rates produce 0.9235. Rate reforms flow through q, including when HI on the incremental draw is off. An explicit factor override is available. The planned adjusted draw q * D_planned must independently meet the existing gov.irs.self_employment.net_earnings_exemption ($400 at current settings). Otherwise actual gross draw D and adjusted earnings N_D are both zero. This independent floor preserves existing SE computations; it is a specific modeling choice rather than an integrated new SECA statute.

Let W be existing taxable wages, S the already adjusted and capped existing social_security_taxable_self_employment_income, and C the preserved cap. For the default cap basis:

room = max(C - W - S, 0)
draw_oasdi_base = min(N_D, room)
added_OASDI = draw_oasdi_base * SECA_OASDI_rate

Wages have priority, then existing SE earnings, then the draw. The independent draw outputs leave the existing SE base intact. Added tax enters the existing SECA liability variables, so payroll and OASDI accounting include it once. HI inclusion adds N_D * SECA_HI_rate without the OASDI ceiling. The deduction uses gov.irs.ald.self_employment_tax.percent_deductible, distinct from the employer-share parameter used to derive q, and retains nondependent aggregation. No second copy of the source income is added to gross income or AGI.

Outputs include owners_draw_active_income, owners_draw_planned_gross, owners_draw_gross, owners_draw_net_earnings_factor, owners_draw_net_earnings, owners_draw_oasdi_room, owners_draw_oasdi_base, owners_draw_oasdi_tax, and owners_draw_hi_tax.

For the current-law cap basis, the taxed draw is nonnegative and no larger than remaining room, adjusted earnings, or actual gross draw. Adding the draw cannot push otherwise capped covered earnings beyond C. Increasing the phase-in share cannot reduce draw tax when other inputs and settings stay fixed. Already-SE income is removed before computing the draw, and disabled years retain baseline tax and deduction behavior. The 1 cap option intentionally permits earnings above C.

Benefit credit and active NIIT interaction¶

owners_draw_credited_earnings exposes N_D when benefit credit is enabled, and zero otherwise. The flag has no tax effect. The reform passes these annual earnings to the existing ss_aime earnings-history helper and adds them before applying each year’s wage-base cap. A supplied positive ss_aime_input retains its baseline precedence. This reform does not append an amount to final AIME or alter reported benefits. This bridge inherits main’s AIME lookback and age rules and its separate gov.ssa.social_security.wage_base path, last authored for 2026; it does not synchronize that path with the current tax cap. Mini-PIA integration and a complete annual-history ledger remain separate future work.

owners_draw_niit_exclusion is the matching gross-profit exclusion for a S.770 active-business NIIT option. With OASDI only it is draw_oasdi_base / q: only profits whose adjusted earnings actually face SECA are excluded. With HI enabled it is the entire actual gross draw D, because N_D faces uncapped HI. Profits beyond OASDI room remain in the active base when HI is off. The undrawn share and any floor-rejected planned draw also remain. Existing already-SE gross K_active must separately be excluded regardless of the OASDI cap because existing SE earnings face uncapped HI. When both reforms are active, s770_active_business_income consumes this calculated exclusion, including an explicit net-factor override. When owners draw is disabled, before its start year, or uninstalled, the S.770 manual draw hooks retain their original behavior.

The automatic joint route requires material_participation_only: true. Passive-inclusive draws remain supported by the standalone owners’ draw reform, but the joint active-business calculation raises an explicit error for that setting: the aggregate draw does not identify which taxed profits belong in the active residual or in NII. Supporting that combination requires a validated source allocation, including NII’s separate s770_nii_fica_seca_income hook. Subtracting the entire passive-inclusive draw from active profits would misallocate the remaining bases between tax rates.

Data limitations¶

The intended field contract is separate S-corporation and partnership totals, with the combined section 469 passive subset subtracted once and the already-SE partnership subset removed without double counting.

Current production exports force s_corp_income to zero and put combined nonpassive S-corporation and partnership donor income into partnership_income. Calibration carries passive income mass into that combined column, while passive_partnership_s_corp_income is absent and defaults to zero. partnership_self_employment_net_earnings is present. An absent passive column does not establish that passive income is zero. This can overstate the active residual and makes population results provisional.

Microcosm #1251 repairs the total/passive contract, and #1261 separates entity totals. Nonprovisional population scoring requires a pinned artifact containing these repairs and a receipt validating the stored columns and their meanings. No population impact is measured by this PR; the hub will measure it.

Comparable OACT provisions¶

OACT’s current coverage provision F12 says, “make all distributions to all pass-through business owners up to the earnings cap subject to the SECA tax”. Its current table starts in 2029 and requires material participation. This reform’s example 2027–2036 phase-in and default OASDI-only treatment are distinct choices.

OACT’s current payroll-tax provisions separate rate increases, taxation above the taxable maximum, and partial taxation above it. The older E5 label belongs to the legacy contribution-and-benefit-base table, which uses the 2008 Trustees assumptions. E5 says, “Raise the taxable maximum amount (the contribution and benefit base) to include 90 percent of total OASDI covered earnings.” Its phased higher cap also earns benefit credit. It is not a current 2026 E5 category. Cap changes and benefit-credit choices are distinct from expanding the owners’ draw base.
