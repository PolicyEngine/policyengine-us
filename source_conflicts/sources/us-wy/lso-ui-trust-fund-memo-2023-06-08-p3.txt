         Unemployment Taxable Wage Base.
         Another driver of total unemployment insurance tax revenue is the taxable wage base specified in
         W.S. 27-3-303(a). This is the amount to which the tax rate applies. The unemployment taxable
         wage base is currently defined in statute as 55 percent of the prior year's statewide average annual
         wage.9 Table 2 shows the individual taxable wage base as calculated by DWS for 2018 to 2023.
         The taxable wage base has increased by approximately 18 percent between 2018 and 2023. While
         Wyoming had no increase in unemployment tax rates for 2023, the taxable wage base increased
         to $29,100 for all employers. Even in the event the rate does not increase from year to year, since
         the base is increasing, the required payment from some employers may still increase, all else being
         equal.
