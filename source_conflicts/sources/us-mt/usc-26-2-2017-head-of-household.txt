United States Code, 2017 edition, 26 USC 2(b)(1).

Operative excerpt (10 words):
is not a surviving spouse (as defined in subsection (a))

Context summary:
The federal head-of-household definition excludes an individual who qualifies as a surviving spouse under subsection (a). This exclusion determines access to the doubled Montana standard deduction through federal head-of-household eligibility.
