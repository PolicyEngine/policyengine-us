Section 2. Transition. The department of revenue shall update the subtraction amount in 15-30-2120(3)(o) for a qualified volunteer firefighter or volunteer emergency care provider pursuant to 15-30-2120(7) by making the inflation adjustment applicable to income tax years beginning after December 31, 2026.
Section 3. Effective date. [This act] is effective January 1, 2026.
Section 4. Applicability. [This act] applies to income tax years beginning after December 31, 2025.
