Montana Code Annotated 2019, 15-30-2132(1).

Operative excerpt (17 words):
single individual who qualifies to file as a head of household on the federal income tax return

Context summary:
Montana doubles the minimum and maximum standard deduction for married joint filers and individuals who qualify for federal head-of-household filing. Montana determines marital status at year end, or at death if a spouse died during that tax year.
