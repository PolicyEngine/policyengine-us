2019 Montana Form 2 instructions, Head of Household, PDF page 10.

Operative excerpt (20 words):
Generally, when your filing status on your federal Form 1040 is Qualifying Widow(er), you can use Head of Household status.

Context summary:
The department generally permits a federal qualifying widow or widower to file Montana head of household. The same section also requires federal head-of-household eligibility and asks the filer to verify qualification against federal instructions.
