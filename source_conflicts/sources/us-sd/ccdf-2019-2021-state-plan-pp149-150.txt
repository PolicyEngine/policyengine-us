           Start-up funding.
          Describe:
          Health and safety grants are available to new providers in need of supplies,
          materials or equipment when starting their new child care program.


           Technical assistance support.
          Describe:
          The Early Childhood Enrichment programs provide technical assistance to
          programs serving or enrolling a child with a disability to support knowledge of the
          disability and care requirements.


           Recruitment of providers.
          Describe:
          Child Care Services has recruitment procedures for areas in need of child care.
          Recruitment of providers for children with disabilities is completed on a case by
          case basis depending on the disability, needs of child, and needs of the family. The
          licensing specialist and the Early Childhood Enrichment program assist in this
          process.


           Tiered payment rates (as discussed in 4.3.2).
          Describe:
