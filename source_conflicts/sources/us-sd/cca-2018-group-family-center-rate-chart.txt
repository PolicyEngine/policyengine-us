Special Needs Rate: $5.00 rate ceiling - all ages, all counties

Providers caring for a child with special needs can be reimbursed up to this maximum rate. In
some instances, a higher rate for the care of a child with special needs can be negotiated. Contact
the Division of Child Care Services at 1-800-227-3020 for additional information.


The Child Care Provider Reimbursement Guide contains complete instructions for receiving
reimbursement from Child Care Services. The guide is available online at:
dss.sd.gov/childcare/childcareassistance. Or you may receive a guide by calling Child Care
Services at 1-800-227-3020.
