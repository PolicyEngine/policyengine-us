         Section 2. This Act takes effect on the date of the last of the following to occur:
              (1) The Division of Revenue has implemented the personal income tax release of the Integrated
    Revenue Administration System.
              (2) The Secretary of Finance provides a written notice to the Registrar of Regulations that the
    contingency in paragraph (1) of this section has been fulfilled.
