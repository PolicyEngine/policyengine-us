Rhode Island 2022 Public Laws, chapter 231, article 13, sections 1 and 4.
R.I. Gen. Laws § 40-5.2-20(f)(2), selected enacted language.

Section 1 changes the continuation limit to "until their incomes exceed three hundred percent (300%) of the applicable federal poverty guidelines."
Section 4 states, "This Article shall take effect July 1, 2022."
