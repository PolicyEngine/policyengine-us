2025 UNEMPLOYMENT INSURANCE
TAX RATES
Effective January 1, 2025, the new employer tax rates
calculated in accordance with Title 24, Chapter 12,
Section 308 of the Virgin Islands Code are as follows:
1. Average Contribution Rate: 4.06% (a decrease from
4.11% in 2024).
2. Employer Rates: Individual rates will vary based on
assigned interval groups, ranging from 2.46% to 5.4%.
3. Visit https://www.vidol.gov/unemployment-insurance/
for more information.
The 2025 rates represent VIDOL’s annual adjustment
process to ensure the stability and sustainability of the
UI Trust Fund. The payroll variation mechanism
introduced last year is working effectively to stabilize
these rates. VIDOL anticiaptes further reductions in
2026 as repayments reduces financial pressures on the
fund.
