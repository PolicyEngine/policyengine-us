ACTNO. 7410

BILL NO. 29-0231

TWENTY-NINTH LEGISLATURE OF THE VIRGIN ISLANDS
Regular Session

2012

An Act amending title 24 Virgin Islands Code, chapter 12, section 308, pertaining to the
employer’s contribution to the Unemployment Insurance Fund and for other purposes

------
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the Virgin Islands:

SECTION 1. Title 24 Virgin Islands Code, chapter 12, section 308 is amended in the
following instances:

1. In subsection (b)(1) by adding a new sentence at the end thereof to read as
follows:

“Each employer, shall pay an amount of $25 per employee, per year to pay the accrued
interest on the Virgin Islands Federal Trust Fund Loan.”

2. In subsection (b)(2) by striking “one percent (1%)” and inserting “two percent
(2%)”;

3. In subsection (b)(8) by striking “zero (0)” and inserting “one and one half percent
(1-1/2%)”. ,

Thus passed by the Legislature of the Virgin Islands on September 18, 2012.
