This report summarizes key data points from state Unemployment Insurance laws and regulations that are effective as of July 1, 2024. This
does not include extensions or special programs. It is one of three regularly provided reports from the Office of Unemployment Insurance (OUI)
Division of Legislation. It may be used in conjunction with the Report on State Legislation and the Comparison of State Unemployment Insur-
ance Laws available online at https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/statelaws.asp#RecentStatelaw. This report is issued solely for informational,
reference, and research purposes. It is not an official interpretation of state UI laws. The state laws and regulations should be consulted for
the full text.
