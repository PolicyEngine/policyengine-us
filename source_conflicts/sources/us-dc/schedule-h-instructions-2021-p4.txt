You must not be claimed as a dependent on
someone else's federal, state, or DC income tax
return unless you reached age 65 on or before
December 31, 2021.

Additional Information:

+ A Homeowner and Renter Property Tax
Credit cannot be claimed on behalf of a
taxpayer who died on or before
December 31, 2021.

* — Only one claimant per "tax filing unit"
can claim the property tax credit.

* An individual who is claimed as a
dependent on someone else's individual
income tax return is eligible to file the
claim for his/her tax filing unit only if the
individual is 65 years of age or older.
