2027 California Premium Subsidy Program Design
Covered California
For plan year 2027, the California Health Benefit Exchange (Exchange) adopts this 2027
Program Design in accordance with item 4800-101-3381 of the Budget Act of 2026 for
implementation of Title 25 of the Government Code. The amount appropriated in item 4800-1013381 of the Budget Act of 2026 shall during the 2027 plan year provide state advance premium
assistance subsidies to the applicable return filers who meet the requirements specified in
subdivision (c).
(a)

The term “state advance premium assistance amount” means, with respect to any
calendar year, the sum of the state premium assistance amounts determined under
paragraph (b) for the applicable return filer’s coverage months during the calendar year.
The monthly state advance premium assistance amount shall not exceed the monthly
premiums for one or more qualified health plans in which the applicable return filer, the
applicable return filer’s spouse, and the applicable return filer’s dependent, if
applicable, enroll through the Exchange.

return filer, the applicable return filer’s spouse, or any dependent (as defined
in Section 17056 of the Revenue and Taxation Code) of the applicable
return filer and which were enrolled in through the Exchange, or
(B) The excess (if any) of:

(i)

For the purposes of this 2027 Program Design, the following definitions shall apply:
(1)

“Actual family size” means the family size, as defined in paragraph (d)(10), that
an applicable return filer reports on the applicable return filer’s State tax return
for the taxable year.

(2)

“Actual filing status” means the filing status an applicable return filer uses to file
the applicable return filer’s State tax return for the taxable year.

(3)

“Actual household income” means the household income, as defined in paragraph
(d)(12), that an applicable return filer reports on the applicable return filer’s State
tax return for the taxable year.

(4)

“Applicable percentage” means the percentage such that the applicable percentage
for any applicable return filer whose household income is within an income tier
specified in the following table shall increase, on a sliding scale in a linear
manner, from the initial premium percentage to the final premium percentage

(5)

“Applicable second lowest cost silver plan” has the same meaning as the term is
defined in subdivision (b)(3)(B) of Section 36B of the Internal Revenue Code and
its implementing regulations in 26 C.F.R. Section 1.36B-3(f).

(6)

“Adjusted monthly premium” has the same meaning as the term is defined in
subdivision (b)(3)(C) of Section 36B of the Internal Revenue Code and its
implementing regulations in 26 C.F.R. Section 1.36B-3(e).

(7)

“Applicable return filer” means, with respect to any month, a return filer whose
household income for the taxable year is at or above 100 percent and at or below
200 percent of the federal poverty level for the return filer's family size.
