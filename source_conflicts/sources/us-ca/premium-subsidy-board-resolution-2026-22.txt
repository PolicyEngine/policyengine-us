COVERED CALIFORNIA
BOARD RESOLUTION NO. 2026-22
In the matter of the approval of the 2027 California Premium Subsidy Program Design:
The Board resolves that, in accordance with Section 100508(a) of the Government
Code, the Board hereby approves and adopts the 2027 California Premium Subsidy
Program Design as presented to the Board on this day, contingent upon the enactment
of the Budget Act of 2026.
*************
CERTIFICATION
I, Jessica Altman, Executive Director Covered California, do hereby certify that the
foregoing action was duly passed and adopted by the Covered California Board at an
official meeting thereof on June 18, 2026.
