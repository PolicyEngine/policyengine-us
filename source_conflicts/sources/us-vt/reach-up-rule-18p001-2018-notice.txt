Rule Number:        18P001
Title:              Reach First / Reach Up Rules.
Type:               Standard
Status:             Adopted
Agency:             Department for Children and Families, Agency of Human Services
Legal Authority:    3 V.S.A. § 801(b)(11); 33 V.S.A. § 105(b)(2); and Act 29 of 2017.
Summary:            This rule implements two changes to the Reach Up program required by Act 29 (2017): (1) increasing the resource limit from $2,000 to $9,000; and (2) excluding funds in retirement accounts and qualified child education savings accounts from consideration as resources. Additionally, this rule modifies provisions in the Reach First and Reach Up program rules related to interviews and orientation, Department of Labor (DOL) reporting requirements, the housing allowance, lump sum payments, matched savings accounts, and time limits. The proposed changes incorporate the concepts of behavioral science, as this field of study relates to how a program's participants behave and make decisions, to make small, incremental changes designed to increase the effectiveness of the program for participants and reduce administrative burden for staff.
Persons Affected:   Families receiving Reach First and Reach Up financial assistance; the Department for Children and Families, Economic Services and Child Development Division; community programs serving Reach Up Clients, including parent child centers, housing programs, and community action agencies.
Economic Impact:    When Act 29 was introduced, the Department had estimated a cost of approximately $250,000 associated with increasing the resource limit and excluding funds in retirement and qualified child savings account. The Department anticipates no additional economic impact from the proposed changes required by Act 29 since these changes were operationalized effective July 1, 2017. The Department anticipates no economic impact from the additional proposed changes as they do not change benefit levels or substantially alter eligibility criteria.
