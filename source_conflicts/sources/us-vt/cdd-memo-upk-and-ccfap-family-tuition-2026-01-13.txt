          Financial Assistance and Universal Prekindergarten

From: Janet McLaughlin, Deputy Commissioner, DCF Child Development Division

Date: January 13, 2026

Re: Clarification regarding payments from family enrolled in both Child Care Financial
Assistance and Universal Prekindergarten

______________________________________________________________________

Purpose
This memo provides clarification of program responsibilities and limitations related to
payments collected from families with children enrolled in both Universal
Prekindergarten (UPK) and the Child Care Financial Assistance Program (CCFAP).

Key Information
      •   Child care programs determine their own tuition rates and enrollment schedules
          according to their own policies. Programs have two main options for invoicing for
          Universal Prekindergarten (UPK):

             o Blended Model: Combine the child care and UPK hours and tuition into a
               single, unified rate.
             o Separate Model: Clearly divide the child care hours and tuition from the
               UPK hours and funding.

          The program must apply the chosen model (blended or separate) consistently to
          all enrolled families and clearly communicate the tuition structure to families.

      •   Invoices provided to families should clearly show: the program’s tuition rate, the
          amount paid by CCFAP, the amount paid by UPK, and the amount due from the
          family after all UPK/CCFAP payments are applied.UPK tuition paid on students’
          behalf must fully cover the cost of 10 hours of UPK for 35 weeks annually. In a
          blended UPK + Child Care program, the UPK tuition payment must be used to
          reduce the amount the family owes.

   •   Families with children participating in UPK or in CCFAP may not be charged
       additional fees due to their participation in either of those programs.

Authority
The following guidance applies to all regulated child care programs, including those
accepting CCFAP:
   •   Licensing regulations (4.7 for both CBCCPP and FCCH) require licensees to
       provide written information on information about fees and payment policies to all
       parents of enrolled children.
   •   Memo-Tuition Rates and Allowability of Additional Fees, including:
         o Child care programs must make their tuition rates available to the public.
   •   CCDF State Plan Section 3.1.2(e): CDD permits child care programs to charge
       CCFAP recipients above the amount of the CCFAP payment and family share,
       but only when the provider’s rate for non-subsidy families exceeds the state
       capped rate.
   •   Understanding CCFAP Payments
         o The amount paid by CCFAP is determined by subtracting the family share
             (if any) from the state rate.
         o The child care program’s tuition rate is set by the program and may differ
             from the state CCFAP rate.
         o The program may offer additional discounts and may choose whether or
             not to collect any family payment.

The following guidance is applicable to regulated child care programs who are approved
to accept UPK tuition:
   •   Memo: Allowable and Not Allowable Use of UPK Tuition (January 2, 2025)
         o The Act 166 public education dollars paid to prequalified programs is to
            offset tuition costs for families whose students are enrolled in prequalified
            prekindergarten education programs.
         o Additional fees, costs, and non-refundable deposits may be charged to
            families who have students receiving child care or education services
            beyond the 10 hours of publicly funded prekindergarten.

   •   Memo: UPK Enrollment Tuition Invoicing:
         o All UPK tuition received by a UPK prequalified program is to fully cover the
            cost of the 10 hours a week for 35 weeks per school district academic
            year.
         o If a child attends the program for additional hours and weeks, the full
            annual UPK tuition must be applied to the family’s UPK costs. Publicly
            funded PreK dollars must offset a family’s tuition cost and must be
            transparent on the family’s invoice.

Questions?
For questions or concerns about UPK tuition, please contact the Early Education team
at AOE.UPK@vermont.gov.
For questions or concerns about CCFAP or the interaction of CCFAP and UPK, please
contact Child Development Division’s CCFAP team. Please send your questions about
CCFAP payments to: ahs.dcfcddccfappayments@vermont.gov.
The Vermont office of First Children’s Finance (FCF) is funded by CDD to offer business
related technical assistance to child care programs. FCF can offer advice and training
on approaches to setting tuition rates for your child care business.
