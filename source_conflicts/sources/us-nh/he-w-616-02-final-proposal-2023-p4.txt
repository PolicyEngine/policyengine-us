CONSENT                                                                  Final Proposal Fixed 12/15/2022 1

 Readopt with amendment He-W 616.02, effective 02-21-2013 (Document #10275), to read as follows:


 He-W 616.02 Eligibility of Qualified Aliens. As long as all other eligibility requirements are met, financial
 assistance shall be provided to qualified aliens as defined in 8 USC 1641(b), under the following conditions:
       (a) The qualified alien entered the United States with a status within the meaning of the term
 “qualified alien” before August 22, 1996; or
       (b) For qualified aliens who enter the United States on or after August 22, 1996, a period of 5 years
 has elapsed since the date of the alien’s entry into the United States with a status within the meaning of the
 term “qualified alien.”


 Adopt He-W 816.02 to read as follows:


 He-W 816.02 Eligibility of Qualified Aliens. As long as all other eligibility requirements are met, medical
 assistance shall be provided to qualified aliens as defined in 8 USC 1641(b), under the following conditions:
       (a) The qualified alien entered the United States with a status within the meaning of the term
 “qualified alien” before August 22, 1996; or
       (b) For qualified aliens who enter the United States on or after August 22, 1996, a period of 5 years
 has elapsed since the date of the alien’s entry into the United States with a status within the meaning of the
 term “qualified alien.”
