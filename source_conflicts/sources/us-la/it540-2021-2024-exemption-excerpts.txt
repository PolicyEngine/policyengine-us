Single-filer table: "for each exemption over one"

LDR directs filers to count themselves on Line 6A and their federal dependents
on Line 6C in every year. Without blind, aged or adoption adjustments, a single
filer with one federal dependent counts two exemptions. LDR uses one exemption for
the filer's personal allowance and adds $1,000 for the child.
