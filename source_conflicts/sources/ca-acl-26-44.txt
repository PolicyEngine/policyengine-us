The purpose of this All County Letter (ACL) is to provide the adjusted schedule of rates
that reflects the California Necessities Index (CNI) increase of 3.59 percent for fiscal
year (FY) 2026-27 effective July 1, 2026. The CNI rate increase is applicable to
out-of-home placements, including those receiving Approved Relative Caregiver and the
Aid to Families with Dependent Children-Foster Care funding. A separate ACL will be
issued specific to the Adoption Assistance Program (AAP), providing a comprehensive
schedule of rates related to AAP and CNI increases, effective July 1, 2026.

INFANT SUPPLEMENT/EXPECTANT PARENT PAYMENT (EPP) INCREASE

The AB 349 (Chapter 143, Statutes of 2025), codified at WIC 11465(c)(5)(B), requires
that, beginning July 1, 2026, the monthly infant supplement paid to parenting foster
youth and caregivers must be adjusted annually by an amount equal to the CNI.
Because WIC 11465(e)(1) requires that the EPP match the infant supplement rate, the
EPP is increased annually by the CNI, as well. Therefore, for FY 26-27, the monthly
infant supplement and EPP will increase by 3.59 percent.

If you have any questions regarding the rates information in this ACL, please contact
RatesPolicy@dss.ca.gov. For information on claiming instructions, questions may be
sent via email to fiscal.systems@dss.ca.gov. For information on payments, questions
may be sent via email to assistance.claims@dss.ca.gov.
