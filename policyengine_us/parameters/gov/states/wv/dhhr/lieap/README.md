# West Virginia regular heating components

The qualified household-size and income-limit components use the FY2026 state
plan and operational manuals, including the adopted October 2026 update for
FY2027. The assistance group includes co-residents sharing
heating costs. Qualified members count in its size; nonqualified members' income
is not excluded. SPM membership approximates the physical sharing group. Prior
assistance in another group can exclude a member, but receipt history is absent
from existing inputs and is not inferred.

The plan specifies 60% of requested-year SMI through size seven, then 150% of
prior-calendar-year FPG from size eight. Nearest-dollar monthly rounding matches
all ten published agency amounts. Applying the guideline before rounding also
matches the manual's size-eleven limit of $8,831; repeatedly adding the web page's
rounded $688 increment gives a conflicting $8,832. The adopted percentage and
manual calculation take precedence over that rounded web shorthand. This is a
numerically validated rounding convention, not an express rounding instruction.
The same calculation matches the current manual's FY2027 monthly limits for
sizes one through eleven. The payment grid's apparent size-twelve maximum of
$10,160 conflicts with the guideline-derived $9,805; payment cells do not replace
the stated income eligibility rule.

Countable income, final eligibility and benefit remain incomplete. The manual's
all-income rule conflicts with some unchecked state-plan categories, including
TANF, general assistance and minor earnings; the acquired sources do not settle
that conflict. Both exclude Social Security and SSI. The FY2026 payment chart
has overlapping first/second income bands and anomalous larger-family cells.
The October 2026 operational manual repeats an overlap in FY2027. The source
library preserves both literal grids and their edition details; the model does
not silently correct them or encode unmodeled eligible benefits as zero.

Only the household-size and income-limit components are verified for FY2026 and
FY2027. Earlier backfilled results are unverified historical estimates; the whole
program is not verified for either year. The full regular-heating goal remains open. Crisis, cooling,
weatherization, equipment and the separate utility discount are out of scope.
