# Pell Grant

## Annual award-year labels

The annual Pell parameters use model year `Y` for award year `Y–Y+1`, which runs from July 1 of `Y` through June 30 of the following year. For example, model year 2017 uses the maximum from the [2017–2018 award schedule](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/dear-colleague-letters/2016-10-18/gen-16-19-subject-2017-2018-federal-pell-grant-payment-and-disbursement-schedules). January 1 parameter dates label the model year; the Department of Education starts the legal award year on July 1.

The maximum parameter records the regular maximum Scheduled Award in US dollars for full-time attendance over a full academic year. This annual convention does not allocate disbursements between calendar years or establish actual receipt. Minimum awards, expected family contributions, the Student Aid Index, attendance costs and enrollment also affect calculated awards. These maxima alone do not reproduce the full payment tables, career-pathway exceptions or year-round awards.
