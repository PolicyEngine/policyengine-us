Implement California's County Children's Health Initiative Program (CCHIP), the separate CHIP population for children in San Francisco, San Mateo, and Santa Clara counties with household income above the Medi-Cal limit and at or below 322 percent of the federal poverty guideline, accounting for immigration status and available employer coverage, and correct three California Medi-Cal interactions it depends on:
  - Count the unborn children of every pregnant member of a California MAGI Medi-Cal household, not only the applicant's own pregnancy (DHCS ACWDL 20-10).
  - Treat California's child and pregnancy Medi-Cal income ceilings as inclusive, so income exactly at 266 or 213 percent of the poverty guideline stays in Medi-Cal rather than moving to CCHIP or the Medi-Cal Access Program.
  - Exclude California pregnant applicants with other health coverage from the CHIP from-conception-to-end-of-pregnancy population, which is the Medi-Cal Access Program (WIC §§ 15833-15834).
