Follow the statute where IRS forms differ: the refundable child tax credit bar applies only to a section 911 exclusion (not the housing deduction alone); a claimable spouse aged 65 or blind on a 2018-2025 joint return must file with income other than earned income over the section 63(c)(5)(A) amount; estate and trust royalties count as EITC investment income; and the dependent care assistance exclusion of a married taxpayer who does not file jointly is limited by the absent spouse's earned income.
