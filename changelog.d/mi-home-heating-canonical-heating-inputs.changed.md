Updated the Michigan home heating credit to use the shared heating inputs. Heating costs default to the primary-fuel bill for SPM units containing one tax unit; callers must supply `heating_expenses` directly to allocate costs across multiple tax units or report exact November-October costs. Legacy person-level costs remain supported when `heating_type` is `UNSPECIFIED`. The heat-in-rent reduction and alternate-credit exclusion now use the heating-specific flag; callers previously using `utilities_included_in_rent` for Michigan must migrate to `heat_expense_included_in_rent` or `mi_home_heating_credit_heat_included_in_rent`.
