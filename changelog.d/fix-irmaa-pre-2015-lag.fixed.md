Households with a member aged 65 or older no longer raise ParameterNotFoundError in 2015 and 2016. Medicare IRMAA reads income from two years before the benefit year, and for 2015 and 2016 that year (2013, 2014) precedes the first year PolicyEngine models, so the IRMAA income is now zero for those benefit years unless provided, instead of computing adjusted gross income for a year with no parameters.
