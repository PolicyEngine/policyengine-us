Give Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals recipients New York's state-funded Medicaid at every age from 2015 until August 1, 2024. From that date the Essential Plan took recipients aged 19 to 64, so the route covers only those under 19 or aged 65 or older. The model does not apply the return of some adults to Medicaid on July 1, 2026. Annual calculations read the August 2024 change from 2025.
