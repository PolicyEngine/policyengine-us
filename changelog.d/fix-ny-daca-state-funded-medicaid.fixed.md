Let Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals recipients pass New York's state-funded Medicaid immigration test at every age from 2015 until August 1, 2024. The Essential Plan was due to take recipients aged 19 to 64 with income up to 250% of the poverty line from that date, and the model takes every recipient at those ages out of the route, so it covers only those under 19 or aged 65 or older. The model does not apply the return of some adults to Medicaid on July 1, 2026. Annual calculations read the August 2024 change from 2025.
