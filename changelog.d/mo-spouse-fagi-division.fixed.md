Divide federal adjusted gross income between spouses on a Missouri combined return the way the Form MO-1040 Line 1 worksheet and 12 CSR 10-2.010 do. Each spouse's own federal adjustments and losses stay in their own column, and capital losses follow 12 CSR 10-2.010(2). From 2024, a negative federal AGI is replaced by zero, and when joint federal AGI is positive the other spouse enters the whole joint amount (12 CSR 10-2.710). A spouse's Missouri AGI can be negative: Line 6 adds the two columns, and only the Line 7 income percentage is floored.
