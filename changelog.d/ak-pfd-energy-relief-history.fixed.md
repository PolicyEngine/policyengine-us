Give Alaska's Permanent Fund Dividend and energy relief the amounts Alaska paid from 2015 to 2021, the model's first modeled years, instead of 2022's $2,622 dividend and $662 energy relief (#5148). The dividend was $2,072 in 2015, $1,022 in 2016, $1,100 in 2017, $1,600 in 2018, $1,606 in 2019, $992 in 2020 and $1,114 in 2021, with no energy relief, as the PFD Division's summary table and annual reports show. From 2027, energy relief is zero until a budget appropriates a payment, since each payment needs its own appropriation, and the 2026 dividend of $1,000 carries forward until a budget sets the next one.
