Montana's federal income tax deduction for spouses filing separately on the same form now attributes federal tax to the spouse who earned the income and caps each spouse at 5,000; the child and dependent care expense deduction phases out on the couple's combined Montana AGI and is halved only for spouses filing separately on the same form; and the elderly homeowner/renter credit counts income without regard to losses. The separate-filing charitable deduction uses the same spouse allocation rate when determining federal itemization and calculating the actual deduction.
