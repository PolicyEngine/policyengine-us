Apply Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana and Maryland rules for a filer who can be claimed as a dependent: no dependent exemptions or credits on a return with a claimable filer outside the claimant filing exception (Iowa dependent credit, Kansas dependent exemption and food sales tax credit child, Louisiana dependent exemption, Maryland dependent and aged-dependent exemptions and child tax credit). Iowa withholds the low-income exemption, the single-filer tax reduction and the alternate tax from a claimed dependent only when the claiming person's net income exceeds the Iowa Code 422.5 limit, using new claimant income and filing status inputs. Kansas counts each filer whom no one else may claim, plus allowable dependents, for its 2021-2023 exemptions, and each such filer plus dependent children under 18 for its food sales tax credit. Maryland's poverty line credit excludes only a filer actually claimed as an exemption on another Maryland return, through a new input, and keeps ordinary earned income; a filer with no allowed Maryland exemption has no poverty level and so no credit; a Maryland filer who can be claimed still gets no personal exemption or childless EITC. Also limit Maryland's aged dependent exemption by federal AGI under Tax-General 10-211(c), as for the personal exemption.
