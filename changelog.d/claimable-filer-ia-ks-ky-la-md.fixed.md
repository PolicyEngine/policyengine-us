Apply Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana and Maryland rules for a filer who can be claimed as a dependent: no dependent exemptions or credits on a return with a claimable filer outside the claimant filing exception (Iowa dependent credit, Kansas dependent exemption and food sales tax credit child, Louisiana dependent exemption, Maryland dependent and aged-dependent exemptions and child tax credit); a claimed Iowa filer gets the low-income exemption only as a single filer with income below $5,000, and no tax reduction; a claimed Kansas filer has no exemptions in 2021-2023; a Maryland filer who can be claimed gets no personal exemption, poverty line credit or childless EITC, and the poverty line credit stays for a spouse who cannot be claimed. Also limit Maryland's aged dependent exemption by federal AGI under Tax-General 10-211(c), as for the personal exemption.
