Fixed the 2020 unemployment compensation exclusion: the $150,000 test no longer subtracts the compensation twice, each spouse on a joint return excludes up to $10,200 of their own compensation, and a dependent's compensation is excluded on the dependent's own return instead of using the filers' exclusion. The tax unit's taxable unemployment compensation now leaves out dependents' compensation in every year, so Indiana's unemployment compensation deduction no longer deducts compensation that is not in the filers' federal AGI.
