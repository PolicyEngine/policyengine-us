Fixed the 2020 unemployment compensation exclusion: the $150,000 test no longer subtracts the compensation twice, each spouse on a joint return excludes up to $10,200 of their own compensation, and a dependent's compensation is excluded on the dependent's own return instead of using the filers' exclusion. The tax unit's taxable unemployment compensation now leaves out dependents' compensation in every year, so Indiana's deduction, California's and Wisconsin's unemployment compensation subtractions and Rhode Island's add-back of the federally excluded amount count only the compensation on the filers' return. A dependent's own estate or trust loss now reduces the modified AGI of the dependent's own return.
