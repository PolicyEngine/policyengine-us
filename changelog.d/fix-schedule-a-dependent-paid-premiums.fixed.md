Count only the head's and spouse's own health insurance premium payments in federal Schedule A medical expenses (IRC 213(a)). A dependent's own payments no longer enter the filers' subtotal, New Mexico's medical deduction, credit and exemption, or New Jersey's medical expense deduction. North Dakota's renter refund keeps them (NDCC 57-02-08.1(5)(c)), and Montana's 2021-2023 line 1 takes out only the filers' premiums.
