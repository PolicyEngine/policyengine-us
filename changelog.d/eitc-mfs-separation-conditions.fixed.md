Require a resident qualifying child and the statutory separation conditions for married separate filers, and for separated head-of-household filers from 2021, to receive the federal EITC, CalEITC, and credits that follow the federal marital rule. A head or spouse marked as separated meets the six-month living-apart condition by default. A new `is_separated_under_decree` input (default false) treats a head or spouse separated under a decree of separate maintenance as unmarried for the EITC, under IRC 7703(a)(2). New Jersey's childless age fallback now requires the federal marital condition. Washington's Working Families Tax Credit no longer meets the childless age test through a dependent's age.
