Order the residential clean energy credit after the Child Tax Credit, as Schedule 8812 Credit Limit Worksheets A and B and Form 5695 do: the CTC's tax-liability limit subtracts that credit only when Credit Limit Worksheet B applies, the credit's own limit subtracts every other non-refundable credit (including the CTC that cannot be refunded), and it no longer precedes the energy efficient home improvement, elderly or disabled, and clean vehicle credits.
