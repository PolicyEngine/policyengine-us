Consolidated the heating expense input family under a canonical `heating_type` enum and a derived `heating_expense` variable; DC, IL, and MA LIHEAP and the MI home heating credits now read `heating_expense` instead of arbitrating between input vocabularies, and state LIHEAP heating-type enums derive from the canonical input. Every heating type has a decided expense column, including the new `wood_expense` and `other_heating_fuel_expense` inputs; solar-primary homes read the electricity bill for supplemental heat. `heating_expense_person` and `heating_cooling_expense` are deprecated as heating-amount inputs but remain as fallbacks.
