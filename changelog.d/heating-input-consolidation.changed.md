Consolidated the heating expense input family under a canonical `heating_type` enum (defaulting to `UNSPECIFIED`) and a pure derived `heating_expense` variable; DC, IL, and MA LIHEAP read `heating_expense` for households with a known heating type, keep their exact pre-canonical arbitration as deprecated adapters for `UNSPECIFIED` households, and derive their state heating-type enums from the canonical input. Every heating type has a decided expense column, including the new `wood_expense` and `other_heating_fuel_expense` inputs; solar-primary homes read the electricity bill for supplemental heat. `heating_expense_person` and `heating_cooling_expense` are deprecated as heating-amount inputs.
