Make Arkansas income tax projections for 2027 and later consistent. The model now derives the minus adjustments from Act 1 of 2026's indexed statutory tables: table (A), the high-income table (B), and the (C) bracket-adjustment rows. Before, the minus adjustments stayed at their 2026 values while the rate thresholds rose, so tax fell by as much as $111.98 as income crossed a 2026 bound. Bracket bounds, the $94,700 high-income threshold, the (B) 2% row top and each (C) row bound are now indexed and rounded to the nearest $100, per A.C.A. § 26-51-201(d)(1), instead of rounded down. The (C) dollar amounts stay fixed.
