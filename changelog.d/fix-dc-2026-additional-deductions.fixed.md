Allow DC filers to claim the federal deductions for qualified tips, qualified overtime, car loan interest and the enhanced senior deduction from 2026 through 2028. Apply DC's itemized deduction phaseout to these amounts for itemizers. Keep the 2026 basic standard deduction at the 2025 amounts and return to the federal standard deduction from 2030. [D.C. Law 26-189](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/laws/26-189), effective October 2, 2026.
