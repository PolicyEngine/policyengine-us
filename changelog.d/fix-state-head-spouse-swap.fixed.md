Make state income taxes independent of which spouse is labelled head: dependents' income on Arkansas, Delaware, Iowa, Mississippi and Montana separate-column returns goes to the spouse with the greater income (an exact tie splits it); Delaware's EITC, dependent care credit and dependent personal credits no longer go to the head on ties; Montana's joint itemized deductions count both spouses' mortgage and investment interest; and an incapacitated head now qualifies for Minnesota's dependent care credit and Montana's dependent care deduction, a surviving-spouse claimant for Missouri's property tax credit, and a totally disabled spouse for Oklahoma's property tax credit.
