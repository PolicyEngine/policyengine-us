Undocumented residents of New York now pass the Medicaid immigration test only during pregnancy and the postpartum period and, from 2024, at age 65 or older. Undocumented New York children under 19 pass the Children's Health Insurance Program's immigration test, and those who meet its other conditions get coverage with no federal share. Undocumented residents of Massachusetts now pass the Medicaid immigration test only during pregnancy and the postpartum period. Undocumented residents of every age in both states passed the Medicaid immigration test from 2015.
