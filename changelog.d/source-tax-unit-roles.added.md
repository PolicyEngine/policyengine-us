Tax-unit roles and filing statuses supplied by a dataset (tax_unit_role_input, filing_status_input) now determine is_tax_unit_head, is_tax_unit_spouse, is_tax_unit_dependent and filing_status, with age ordering and the filing rules as the fallback. On the certified Populace default build this replaces age-ordered roles with the build's own: adult students and other adult dependents are no longer paired with a parent as joint filers, couples are headed by the build's head, and lone minors head their own returns. In 2024, joint returns fall by 3.07 million and federal income tax by $0.94 billion net. Because the model computes no return for a tax-unit dependent, the income of adults the build makes dependents no longer counts toward any return, which lowers adjusted gross income by $35.9 billion.
