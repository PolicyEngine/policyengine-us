Tax-unit roles and filing statuses supplied by a dataset (tax_unit_role_input, filing_status_input) now determine is_tax_unit_head, is_tax_unit_spouse, is_tax_unit_dependent and filing_status, with age ordering and the filing rules as the fallback. On the certified Populace default build this replaces age-ordered roles with the build's own, so adult students and other adult dependents are no longer paired with a parent as joint filers, couples are headed by the build's head, and lone minors head their own returns; federal and state tax, credit and filing-status totals over that build change accordingly.
