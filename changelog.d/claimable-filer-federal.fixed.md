Apply the federal rules for a filer who can be claimed as a dependent on another return: no child tax credit, credit for other dependents or under-13 care qualifying child on such a return (IRC 24, 21, 152(b)(1)), no personal exemption for that filer (IRC 151(d)(2)) in the exemption count Rhode Island, Delaware, Ohio, Wisconsin, Oregon, Hawaii and New Mexico read, no recovery rebate for that filer, and no education credits or student loan interest deduction on a return with a claimed filer (IRC 25A(g)(3), 221(c)).
