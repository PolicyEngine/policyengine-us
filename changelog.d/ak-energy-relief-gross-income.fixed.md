Count Alaska's energy relief payment in federal gross income from 2023 on, through a new `taxable_ak_energy_relief` source (#9919). IRS Publication 525 for 2024 taxes the entire $1,702 dividend, energy relief included, and the Permanent Fund Dividend Division reports the $1,200 dividend for 2026 the same way. The 2022 payment stays out, because IR-2023-23 says the IRS will not challenge its exclusion.
