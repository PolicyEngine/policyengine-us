Count Alaska's energy relief payment in federal gross income from 2024 on, through a new `taxable_ak_energy_relief` source (#9919). In Publication 525 for 2024, the IRS treats the entire $1,702 dividend as taxable, including the energy relief. The Permanent Fund Dividend Division says the same of the $1,200 dividend for 2026. Payments from the 2022 program stay out, whether recipients got them in 2022 or in 2023. In IR-2023-23, the IRS said it would not challenge a taxpayer who left out a payment that Alaska made in 2022. In section 4.03 of Notice 2023-56, it let a taxpayer who got nothing under the program in 2022 exclude a payment they got in 2023. A payment that a dataset or user supplies counts wherever the recipient lives.
