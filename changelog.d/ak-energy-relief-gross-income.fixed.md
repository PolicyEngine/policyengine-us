Count Alaska's energy relief payment in federal gross income from 2024 on, through a new `taxable_ak_energy_relief` source (#9919). IRS Publication 525 for 2024 taxes the entire $1,702 dividend, energy relief included, and the Permanent Fund Dividend Division reports the $1,200 dividend for 2026 the same way. Payments from the 2022 program stay out, whether received in 2022 or in 2023: IR-2023-23 says the IRS will not challenge their exclusion, and section 4.03 of Notice 2023-56 extends that to payments under the program received in 2023. A supplied payment counts wherever the recipient lives now.
