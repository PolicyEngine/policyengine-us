Count Alaska's energy relief payment in federal gross income from 2024 on, through a new `taxable_ak_energy_relief` source (#9919). In Publication 525 for 2024, the IRS treats the entire $1,702 dividend as taxable, energy relief included, and the Permanent Fund Dividend Division reports the $1,200 dividend for 2026 the same way. Payments from the 2022 program stay out, whether recipients got them in 2022 or in 2023: the IRS said in IR-2023-23 that it would not challenge their exclusion, and in section 4.03 of Notice 2023-56 it extended that to payments under the program that taxpayers got in 2023. A payment that a dataset or user supplies counts wherever the recipient lives.
