Apply Idaho's 2025 eligibility tests to military retirement benefits (classified as disabled, age 62 or older, or working enough to file a federal return) and count them within the retirement benefits deduction's cap and Social Security offset, under Idaho Code 63-3022A as amended by House Bills 40 and 479. The employment test measures income from employment as federal gross income. In every year, Idaho's disability classification (Idaho Code 63-3022A(4)) now also counts Social Security disability insurance benefits and a classification supplied as `DISABLED`, for the age-62 test of the other retirement benefits and, before 2025, of military retirement benefits.
