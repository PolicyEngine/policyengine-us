Let self-employed people whose SEP, SIMPLE or qualified plan is established under a farm (Schedule F) or partnership (K-1 box 14 code A) business deduct their contributions, by default from the combined earned income of every profitable self-employment business under one dollar limit. With retirement plans under several businesses, the self-employed health insurance limit (Form 7206 line 9) subtracts only the part of the plan deduction above the other plan businesses' earned income, the split that leaves the most earned income for that deduction.
