With the abolish-federal-income-tax or abolish-payroll-tax parameter on, household tax and net income now count every other tax: the Idaho permanent building fund tax, the California, Indiana, North Carolina, Oklahoma and Pennsylvania use taxes, and every local income and occupational tax (only New York City's was counted). Pennsylvania refundable credits are no longer counted twice, and federal refundable credits leave household net income in every year the federal income tax is abolished, including when the parameter is first set after 2024.
