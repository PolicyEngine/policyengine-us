The abolish-federal-income-tax and abolish-payroll-tax reforms now build their household totals from the baseline totals less the abolished tax. Under either reform, household tax and net income again include the Idaho permanent building fund tax, the California, Indiana, North Carolina, Oklahoma and Pennsylvania use taxes, and every local income and occupational tax (only New York City's was counted); Pennsylvania refundable credits are no longer counted twice; and the two reforms combine when both are applied.
