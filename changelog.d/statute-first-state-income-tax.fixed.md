Follow the statute where state forms differ: New Mexico's senior medical exemption needs expenses over $28,000; Vermont's 2021 low-income child care credit needs AGI under the threshold; the New York and New York City household credits count the federal exemptions of married, head-of-household and surviving-spouse filers instead of denying the credit when a filer can be claimed (Tax Law 606(b), 1310(d)), and the New York City credit for a married couple filing separately uses the couple's AGI and exemptions; and Puerto Rico's earned income credit uses Hacienda's 2024 and 2025 inflation-adjusted schedules.
