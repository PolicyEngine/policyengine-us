South Carolina TANF now counts Social Security, unemployment compensation, pensions and interest at their gross amounts, adds the unearned income the TANF Policy Manual counts (child support, alimony, veterans benefits, workers' compensation, disability insurance, survivor and railroad retirement benefits, cash contributions), drops retirement account distributions and discharged debt, treats farm income as earned, excludes the first $400 a year of a benefit group's interest and dividends, and excludes SSI recipients' income.
