Compute the federal and California dependent care assistance exclusions and taxable dependent care benefits from Form 2441 line 15 (FTB 3506 line 16): the line 12 benefits in `dependent_care_employer_benefits`, plus grace-period carryovers, less amounts forfeited or carried forward. Forfeited and carried-forward benefits are no longer taxed. Grace-period carryovers count, except that the federal calculation leaves them out in 2021 and 2022, as before; California counts them in every year, and Alabama's 2021 recompute under the 2020 Code counts them.
