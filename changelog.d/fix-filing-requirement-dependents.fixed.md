Stop testing every filer's unearned income against $500 in the federal filing requirement (26 U.S.C. 6012): the unearned income test now applies only to a filer who can be claimed as a dependent on another return, uses the section 63(c)(5)(A) amount for the year ($1,350 in 2025) plus that filer's additional standard deduction for age or blindness, and counts gross income other than wages and self-employment income, so a self-employment loss no longer counts as unearned income.
