Decide New Mexico Premium Assistance's income edges on the exact ratio of ACA MAGI to the prior-year poverty guideline instead of the truncated `aca_magi_fraction`. Incomes from 400.003% to 400.994% of the poverty line were in neither component (the base program's federal gate ends at 400%, and the Middle Income Household gate needed a truncated fraction above 4.00); they now qualify as Middle Income Households (Addendum 1: "above 400% of the FPL"). The PY2026 10% benchmark gross-up no longer applies from 200.003% to 200.994% ("up to 200% FPL"), and the base program's 400% ceiling holds on its own under a reform that extends the federal credit.
