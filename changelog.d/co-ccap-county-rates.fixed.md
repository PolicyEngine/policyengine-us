Cap Colorado's Child Care Assistance Program payment for each eligible child at the county's reimbursement rate for that child's care, from CDEC's state-established licensed rates (October 2024 and October 2025) and each county's qualified exempt rates, and cap the parent fee at the reimbursement. The provider absorbs charges above the rate, so `childcare_expenses` leaves the family the capped fee for an eligible child's care. The model had paid any charge in full.
