Utah's military retirement credit no longer goes unclaimed when the filer has neither a retirement credit nor a Social Security benefits credit; the retirement-credit choice now weighs the military credit alongside the Social Security credit; the credit uses the income tax rate, as 59-10-1043 requires (4.45% in 2026), starting in 2021; and the credit now reduces Utah tax for 2021 through 2023, which 2021 S.B. 11 covers but the nonrefundable credit list left out.
