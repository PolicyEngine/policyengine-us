The model now computes Colorado's Child Care Assistance Program parent fee under 8 CCR 1403-1 section 3.124. Each household pays one fee: 1% of gross income plus 14% of income above the poverty guideline Colorado sets each October, and $15 for each child in care after the first when income exceeds that guideline. The model drops the cents, charges 55% of the fee when every child in care receives part-time care, and takes 20% off at a provider in the top three quality levels. A household whose children have no recorded hours of care now pays the full-time fee, where the model used to charge nothing. From October 2028 the model applies the marginal rates of section 3.124(Q).
