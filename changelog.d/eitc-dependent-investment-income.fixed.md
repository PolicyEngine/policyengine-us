The EITC investment income test (26 U.S.C. 32(i)) now counts only the head's and spouse's interest, dividends, capital gains, rents and passive income. A tax unit dependent's income no longer denies the credit, and a dependent's losses no longer offset the filer's income. A negative capital gain distributions input is floored at zero for each filer, as in adjusted gross income, instead of offsetting other capital gains.
