California's non-MAGI Medi-Cal asset limit falls to $21,000 for one person and $31,000 for two on July 1, 2027 (SB 164) in the aged, blind and disabled, medically needy, Medicare Savings Program and 250% Working Disabled Program limits; the Working Disabled Program also adds $1,550 for each person beyond two. The aged, blind and disabled limit also gains its missing July 1, 2022 step to $130,000 / $195,000.
