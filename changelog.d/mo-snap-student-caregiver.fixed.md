SNAP student child-care exception: each child under six exempts only one adult in states whose manuals say so (AK, CA, CO, CT, GA, KS, NC, TX, WA), and Missouri and Illinois cap the claim at one adult per household (MO DSS SNAP Manual § 1135.025.00, IL WAG 03-04-03-b); other states keep the uncapped federal reading. The claim goes first to student parents who have no other exception. The non-parent student exceptions move into a new `meets_snap_non_parent_student_exception` variable.
