SNAP student child-care exception: each child under six exempts only one adult in states whose manuals say so (AK, CA, CO, CT, GA, KS, NC, TX, WA), Missouri and Illinois cap the claim at one adult per household, and other states keep the uncapped federal reading; the claim goes first to student parents with no other exception and stays within the child's tax unit; Illinois, Washington and Alaska presume a non-student parent who is not working provides the care. The non-parent student exceptions move into `meets_snap_non_parent_student_exception`.
