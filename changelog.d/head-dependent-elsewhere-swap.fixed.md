The federal standard deduction and the New York, Michigan, Oregon, Virginia, Missouri, New Mexico, Maine and Hawaii rules for a filer claimed as a dependent elsewhere no longer depend on which spouse is labelled head; each follows its jurisdiction's rule for a joint return where one spouse can be claimed. New York's $3,100 dependent standard deduction now applies only to single filers, and Hawaii now limits the standard deduction of a filer who can be claimed as a dependent.
