Count strike and lockout benefits other than gifts in federal gross income and as earned income, and carry them through the state income tax bases that list federal income sources, except Pennsylvania's, which leaves them out of taxable income and counts them in Tax Forgiveness eligibility income; Utah, Montana and Arizona property relief now count only the gift part as nontaxable income. Leave strike benefits out of the kiddie tax support test and the federal and California AMT child exemptions. Count an adult dependent's own adjusted gross income in Utah homeowner's and renter's relief household income, and leave out the income of a parent or grandparent of the claimant or spouse. Net a dependent's estate and trust losses in the modified adjusted gross income for their taxable Social Security and IRA deduction.
