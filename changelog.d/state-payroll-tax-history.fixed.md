Give state employee payroll taxes their published 2018-2027 histories. Connecticut Paid Leave and the Oregon statewide transit tax no longer run back before contributions began (January 2021 and July 2018). Massachusetts PFML (from October 2019), New Jersey TDI and FLI, New York Paid Family Leave (from 2018) and Rhode Island TDI take each year's published rate and wage base for 2018-2023, where they read zero. Massachusetts PFML, the Oregon transit tax and the Vermont Child Care Contribution average the rates in force at the four quarter starts, so a partial first year counts only its months in force.
