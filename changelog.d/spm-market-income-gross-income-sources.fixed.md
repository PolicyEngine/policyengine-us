Count partnership and S corporation income, farm rental income, capital gain distributions reported without Schedule D, Form 4797 gains and losses, and employer-plan disability benefits in person `market_income`, and so in SPM resources, since `spm_unit_taxes` already subtracted the tax on them (#9919). Household market income now also counts the last three, so the person and household lists match except for discharged debt. A new `other_net_gain_person` splits the tax unit's Form 4797 net gain or loss between the head and spouse. Programs whose income adds `market_income` (North Dakota's renter's refund, Washington's ECEAP and Missouri's Supplemental Nursing Care) and the ranking that picks which adults get a simulated marginal tax rate now see these sources too.
