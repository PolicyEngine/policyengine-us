Count only the head's and spouse's own expenses in federal itemized deductions: real estate taxes in the SALT deduction and capped_property_taxes, mortgage and investment interest, charitable contributions (including the non-itemizer deduction), gambling losses and winnings, pre-2018 miscellaneous expenses, and the investment interest the pre-2026 itemized deduction limitation excludes. A dependent's own amounts belong on the dependent's return. Puerto Rico's mortgage interest deduction likewise counts only the filers' interest. State formulas that rebuild or subtract these federal amounts (California, Colorado, Delaware, Idaho, Missouri, New Mexico, New York, South Carolina, Utah and Virginia) and the WATCA contrib reform use the same filer-only sums.
