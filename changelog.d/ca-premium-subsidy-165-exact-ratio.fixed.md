Decide California Premium Subsidy eligibility on the exact ratio of nonnegative ACA MAGI to prior-year FPL instead of the truncated `aca_magi_fraction`, so PY2026 incomes from 165.003% to 165.994% of FPL are no longer eligible ([2026 Program Design, (d)(7)](https://board.coveredca.com/meetings/2025/July%2028,%202025/CoveredCA_2026_Premium_Subsidy_Program_Design_Final.pdf#page=2): "at or below 165 percent"). The PY2027 limit becomes a strict 200% ([2027 Program Design V4, (d)(7)](https://board.coveredca.com/meetings/2026/May%2021,%202026/CoveredCA_2027_Premium_Subsidy_Program_Design_V4_05-19-26.pdf#page=2): "under 200 percent"), which leaves PY2027 eligibility unchanged.
