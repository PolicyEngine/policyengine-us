Apply Arizona, Arkansas and California rules for a filer who can be claimed as a dependent. A return on which the filer (or, if joint, either spouse) can be claimed counts no dependents for Arizona's dependent credit, family credit and 2021 families rebate, Arkansas's dependent credit, disabled-dependent credit (except for a filer's own child) and low-income tables, or California's dependent exemption credit, and gets no childless CalEITC or foster youth credit. A claimable filer loses California's personal, senior and blind credits and is not counted for Arizona's excise credit. California's standard deduction follows its dependent worksheet, and its renter credit excludes a claimable filer who lived in the claimer's home (new input `lives_with_claiming_taxpayer`). California's 2025 joint renter credit income limit is now $107,988.
