Count the housing subsidy a household receives toward CA CPUC CARE/FERA income, in place of the SPM's capped housing subsidy. PG&E Form 01-9077 defines total gross annual household income as "all taxable and non-taxable revenues from all people living in the home, from whatever sources derived, including, but not limited to, ... housing and military subsidies", with no netting against rent and no cap; the SPM cap made CARE eligibility depend on which SPM geography a request selected.
