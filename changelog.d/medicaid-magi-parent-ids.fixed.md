Use optional parent identifiers (`parent_1_id`, `parent_2_id`) for Medicaid MAGI household composition. In households that supply them, each non-filer's household counts the applicant, a co-resident spouse, their own children under the age limit and, for a child-age applicant, co-resident parents and child-age siblings, each once, and California's unborn-child count follows the same members. A tax dependent in such a household whose household is the claiming taxpayer's also counts, once, a co-resident spouse that household does not already include. `is_parent` combines the child count with the links, and the claimed-by-parent test resolves ids within the claiming tax unit, including a known claiming tax unit elsewhere, unless an id names a co-resident. Simulations in which every parent identifier is zero keep their existing outputs. A household without identifiers keeps its composition formulas, but a tax unit or family shared with a linked household can change who is presumed a parent.
