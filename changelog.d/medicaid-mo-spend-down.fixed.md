Let aged, blind and disabled people in Missouri who do not receive SSI deduct incurred medical expenses to reach the state's income standard, as 42 CFR 435.121(e)(4) requires in a section 209(b) state with no medically needy program. A child's 209(b) spend down now also counts the parents' medical expenses. In Missouri, a child's resource test now counts the parents' entire resources, and nonblind disabled children are covered.
