Stop a tax unit dependent's pension, IRA, 401(k), military or CSRS retirement income from reducing the filer's state income tax in Connecticut, Hawaii, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, New York, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, Vermont and West Virginia. Federal AGI already leaves out dependents' income, so these state subtractions now count only the head's and spouse's amounts.
