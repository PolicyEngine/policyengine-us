Stop a tax unit dependent's pension, IRA, 401(k), military or CSRS retirement income from reducing the filer's state income tax in Connecticut, Hawaii, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, New York, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, Utah, Vermont and West Virginia. Federal AGI already leaves out dependents' income, so these state subtractions, and Utah's military retirement credit, now count only the head's and spouse's amounts.
