Keep every baseline benefit in SPM resources under the Texas rebate, the End Child Poverty Act, its Economic Dignity for All Agenda version and the BOOST Act. Each reform copied the `spm_unit_benefits` program list, and the copies had fallen behind: Texas dropped 19 state SSI supplements, and the three Tlaib reforms dropped 42 supplements, Washington's State Family Assistance and Refugee Cash Assistance and the Washington and West Virginia child care subsidies, and counted ACP and EBB twice. The list now lives in the `gov.household.spm_unit_benefits` parameter, and each reform adds its own benefit there and to `gov.household.household_benefits` with the new `add_to_list_parameter` helper. The End Child Poverty Act and BOOST Act household totals now count CSFP and household health benefits too. A new `boost_middle_class_tax_credit_paid_through_ssa` variable holds the BOOST credit when the Social Security Administration pays it. A new check in `test_aggregate_list_integrity.py` fails when a reform module reads or changes a benefit list parameter and `test_reform_benefit_lists.py` does not check that reform's lists against the baseline.
