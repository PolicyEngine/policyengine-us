Missouri MHABD (and New Hampshire APTD): the 209(b) SSI-recipient pathway screens for substantial gainful activity, sparing section 1619(a) recipients whose own income after the SSI exclusions (including the student earned income exclusion) leaves SSI cash payable; the income standard is the individual or married-couple guideline (Appendix J) and a married couple's countable income is summed across the marital unit whatever their filing status; parental income is deemed to a disabled child in the SSI exclusion order (DSS Manual § 0805.020.15); regression tests for stepparent exclusion and equal split of deemed parental income.
