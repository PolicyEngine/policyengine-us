Follow the statute for four credits and deductions: the NYC school tax credit rate reduction amount uses the fixed $37, $25 and $21 second-band bases (Tax Law § 606(ggg)(4-b)); the Indiana supplemental homestead credit requires homestead standard deduction qualification and uses the homestead's liability net of referendum taxes (IC 6-1.1-20.6-7.7); the Virginia Commonwealth Savers deduction caps each account separately and carries forward undeducted contributions (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03(7)); and the Washington senior and disabled persons property tax exemption requires retirement by reason of disability or the VA-rated veteran pathway instead of generic disability (RCW 84.36.381(3)(a)).
