Deem income to a disabled child from a disabled parent whose own income, resources or noncitizen status rule out SSI, as 20 CFR 416.1160(d) defines an ineligible parent; limit deeming to the parents that parent ids name and their spouses; and subtract the couple rate for a parent living with a spouse, so nothing reaches the child while that spouse stays eligible (20 CFR 416.1165(d)(3), 416.1166(e)(1)). In the 12 states where the Social Security Administration administers the state supplement, a parent is not treated as ineligible on income alone, and a parent's spouse who files separately has their income counted with the parent's.
