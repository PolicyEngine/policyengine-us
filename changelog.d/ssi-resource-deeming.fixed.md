Deem SSI resources from co-resident spouses and parents. A spouse is the other member of a two-person marital unit, or the other head or spouse of one's tax unit within a shared marital unit, never a tax dependent of the other; the same test decides whether a child is married. Parents are named by optional parent links, or else found by a tax-unit proxy over is_parent that cannot tell a grandparent from a parent (as in microsimulation data without parent links), and each parent's spouse counts wherever they file; the excess over the reformable allowance is divided equally among the eligible children who share those parents, with redistribution. A spouse's resources count against the couple limit whenever the claim is not already joint, so some applicants with a low-resource spouse newly pass while others fail; immigration-ineligible children do not share parental resources. These eligibility changes also affect SSI-linked state benefits and Medicaid pathways.
