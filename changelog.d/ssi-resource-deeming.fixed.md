Deem SSI resources from co-resident spouses and parents. A spouse is the other member of a two-person marital unit, or the other head or spouse of one's tax unit within a shared marital unit; when a simulation has a single marital unit (as when marital units are omitted), a tax dependent is never the spouse of the person claiming them. The same test decides whether a child is married. Parents are named by optional parent links, or else found by a tax-unit proxy over is_parent that cannot tell a grandparent from a parent (as in microsimulation data without parent links), and each parent's spouse counts wherever they file. The excess over the reformable allowance is divided equally among the children who share those parents and are not already ineligible on citizenship or their own income, with redistribution. A spouse's resources count against the couple limit whenever the claim is not already joint, with ineligible-spouse exclusions only for a spouse who is not aged, blind or disabled; some applicants with a low-resource spouse newly pass while others fail. These eligibility changes also affect SSI-linked state benefits and Medicaid pathways.
