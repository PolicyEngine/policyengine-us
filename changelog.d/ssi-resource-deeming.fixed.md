Deem SSI resources from co-resident ineligible spouses and parents, identifying a spouse the same way for both (a married tax unit sharing a marital unit, a two-person marital unit with cohabitation recorded, or two dependents of one tax unit sharing a marital unit), using optional parent links across tax units, reformable allowances, and eligible-child allocation with redistribution. Spousal deeming applies the couple limit, so some applicants with a low-resource spouse newly pass while others fail; immigration-ineligible children do not share parental resources. These eligibility changes also affect SSI-linked state benefits and Medicaid pathways.
