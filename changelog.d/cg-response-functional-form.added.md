Add semi-elasticity and net-of-tax-elasticity forms for the capital gains realization response, selected by setting `gov.simulation.capital_gains_responses.semi_elasticity` or `net_of_tax_elasticity`. The semi-elasticity form is defined at a 0% baseline rate and puts the revenue-maximizing rate at 1 / semi-elasticity; JCT's 1990 coefficient of 3.51 gives its published 28.5%. The existing log-rate `elasticity` form is unchanged.
