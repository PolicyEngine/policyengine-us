### Methodology choices for Max

Washington rounding is resolved: follow the [agency's published standards](https://www.hca.wa.gov/assets/free-or-low-cost/income-standards-20260101.pdf), which [WAC 182-513-1385(2)](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=182-513-1385) expressly identifies. The January $2,850 allowance differs by $0.625 from bare federal arithmetic; the model discloses that difference and follows Max's d1011 precedent.

The remaining choices are:

- **Standalone equity gate.** Choice: retain an independent statutory home-equity payment-bar screen, with the modeled-pathway gate in composite eligibility. Alternative: make the standalone output false whenever the financial pathway is unmodeled. The chosen design preserves a useful equity result independently of which income/resource route is implemented. [42 USC §1396p(f)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/1396p#f) fixes the legal payment bar and exceptions; it does not prescribe this software API boundary. This is an API design choice, with no discretion to waive the statutory bar.
- **Delaware couple budgeting.** Choice: the caller supplies the legally permitted unit and its correctly attributed totals.
